CBU's 1st Year SICT Tutors

Get help in MA110, PH110, CS150 and CS130 from senior students who have been through them.

Unofficial, student-run service. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Copperbelt University.

How it works

  1. Pick your course and choose a tutor.
  2. Tap "Request this tutor". WhatsApp opens with your request already written.
  3. We confirm the time with the tutor and message you back.
  4. Meet your tutor in a public place on campus.

Find a tutor

Tutors are senior students. Always meet in a public place on campus and do not pay large amounts in advance.
